# Regional Totals Summary Report

## Overview
This report summarizes the financial records across three regions: North, South, and West.

## Summary Statistics

| Region | Total Amount (cents) | Record Count |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|
| North  | 328,507              | 8            |
| South  | 319,239              | 8            |
| West   | 499,228              | 8            |
| **Total** | **1,146,974**      | **24**       |

## Data Processing
All 24 records (R001 through R024) were successfully processed. Each record contained a region identifier and an amount in cents. The totals were calculated by aggregating all amounts within each region.

## Key Findings
- The West region has the highest total amount at 499,228 cents
- The South region has the lowest total amount at 319,239 cents
- Records are evenly distributed across the three regions (8 records per region)
- Combined regional total: 1,146,974 cents
